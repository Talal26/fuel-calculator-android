This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

This Privacy Policy will be updated if the data practices of the app change.

The current version of the app does not require third-party online services to provide its core functionality.

If third-party services, including analytics providers, are added in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe their use and the types of information they may collect.

Any information entered into the app is used to provide its functionality. The app does not currently transmit this information to the developer.

The app is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.


Changes to This Privacy Policy
